# Remaining proof obligations

This is the completion list for the request to prove the project's mathematics
and physics. A passing sampled test is not a completed universal proof. A
counterexample resolves a proposed claim as false, rather than proving it true.
No row is waived because it is expensive or difficult.

The mathematical statements must distinguish exact real models, finite-precision
implementations, source correspondence, and empirical physical calibration.
The current renderer is a visualization with prescribed plasma and binary
motion. Verifying those prescriptions cannot establish that they are exact
physical solutions.

The user delegated the reference and accuracy choices. [TARGETS.md](TARGETS.md)
now fixes the exterior domain, finite affine duration, trajectory/transfer
error targets and the physical comparison suite. These are goals to verify;
they are not assumptions that the current implementation meets them.

## N2 details

[n2-certificates.md](n2-certificates.md) describes the certified-ray
theorems, the step checker and the executable. The a priori results below
remain valid. Their constants are existential, and by `RayGrowth` no a priori
bound reaches the TARGETS.md accuracy, which is why N2 is discharged per ray:

- `KerrSmoothness` and `KerrCompact`: smoothness of the actual
  Hamiltonian/screen field, plus uniform derivative and Lipschitz bounds on
  compact positive-radius phase regions.
- `KerrDerivativeBounds` and `RayGrowth`: explicit first-derivative and field
  growth bounds in the normalized exterior.
- `UniformRemainder.uniform_small` and `RK4Consistency.kerr_uniform_step_bounds`:
  a uniform local defect C h⁵ on compact exterior regions.
- `KerrField.compact_continuation`: continuation while a solution stays in such
  a region.
- `RK4Consistency.kerr_certified_global_error` and
  `kerr_certified_variable_error`: fixed- and variable-step accumulation with
  initial error and roundoff.

Completion means replacing each open entry with a checked theorem tied to its
source and hypotheses, or a checked counterexample for a false statement.
External assumptions and empirical targets must remain visible. They cannot be
converted into project axioms or marked proved by adding more sampled tests.
